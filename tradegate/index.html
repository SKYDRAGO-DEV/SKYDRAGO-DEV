03 / RESEARCH METHODOLOGY

Universal rules. Different evidence.

01

Risk first

A valid stop, contract value, modeled costs, conservative sizing and explicit account limits are prerequisites—not signs of profit.

02

Method-specific proof

Price action needs objective setups. Quant needs OOS validation. L2 needs venue-aligned data. Bots need kill switches and forward tests.

03

Execution reality

Stop prices are not guaranteed fills. Market liquidity, slippage, latency, margin and broker rules can invalidate any simple model.
